Tales of the lost and the desolate garner hope for triumph of souls to be.

No longer does the fall equate to praise but the landing and subsequent rise do.

Strength lies above.

Lift to the heavens for heavenly powers beckon it, not from the pages of gospel
but the eyes of faith and struggle firsthand.

Inspire from within, be the greater version of what no one expects.

Exceed the reaches of your vision for above those limits your true
potential climbs.
