Pause and consider where the winds will take you, sails don’t lead the
way they catch the direction.

Understand the sea is for no one man alone but the waters travel in
solitude just fine.

Trek for miles on end, sun and moon alike will guide you from above,
stars by your side.

Look forward to the waters ahead, don’t question your mast but simply
let the weather pass.

Be strong, unbending to what you confront, for the seas have vanquished
some but not all.

The port is ahead but remember for each port another journey awaits.
Sail strong forever more.
