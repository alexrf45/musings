Pause. Hold. Stop. Fret. Question. Doubt. Ponder. Help. Assist. Tout.
Assess. Yell. Whisper. Pout. Wisp. Sniff. Look. Blink. Inhale. Exhale.
Pull. Push. Grin. Laugh. Cry. Weigh. Walk. Jog. Answer. Place. Take.
Hope. Dream. Imagine. Pick. Choose. Decide. Infer. Concur. Deliberate.
Inspire. Delegate. Process. Think. Grade. Benchmark. Examine. Comb.
Wash. Soak. Simmer. Hesitate. Juke. Jive. Dance. Prance. Complain.
Order. Decline. Draw. Evaluate. Promise. Fold. Open. Close. Propose.
Deem. Select. Concentrate. Emancipate. Deflate. Inflate. Check. Monitor.
Lift. Drop. Handle. Angle. Slide. Broker. Deal. Hassle. Veto. Commit.
Commentate. Procreate. Deviate. Toss. Temper. Tamper. Prepare. Delve.

Wait. Decisive action sometimes comes from waiting for the right moment,
something we ourselves cannot truly predict but only realize at that
exact moment what our perception of reality has allowed us to visualize
and allocate to a particular venue of cognitive delegation and mental
facilitation AKA taking action, making a decision.

So wait.
